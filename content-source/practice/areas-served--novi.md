## Personal Injury Law Firm Serving Novi, MI

Novi is a city of about 67,000 in western Oakland County where I-96, I-275, and M-5 meet, and where the Twelve Oaks Mall, the Novi Town Center and Fountain Walk retail, the Vibe Credit Union Showplace (formerly Suburban Collection Showplace) expo center, and the hotel cluster around the I-96/Novi Road interchange draw traffic from the whole region. West of Beck Road, the warehouses and distribution centers that have built out toward Wixom put tractor-trailers on I-96 and the mile roads; north and south, Novi Road, Beck, Haggerty, Meadowbrook, Taft, and the mile roads carry the subdivisions to the freeways. In a serious injury case, the available liability insurance and the client’s own underinsured motorist coverage may both affect the recovery. In 2024, Novi recorded 1,984 traffic crashes, with 579 people injured and three killed.

## Where Novi injuries happen

**I-96 and the Novi Road interchange.** I-96 across the city from the Farmington Hills line to Wixom, with the Haggerty/M-5, Novi Road, Beck, and Wixom Road interchanges. The Novi Road interchange serves Twelve Oaks, the Town Center, the hotels, and the Showplace, and its ramps and the signals on Novi Road at Twelve Mile and Crescent carry the merging and left-turn crashes of a regional retail destination; the Beck Road interchange also carries commuter and warehouse traffic. Freight to the Beck and Wixom Road warehouses uses the western interchanges. I-96 is MDOT’s. See our [Novi truck accident page](/personal-injury/truck-accidents).

**I-275 and M-5.** I-275 along the city’s eastern edge to the I-96/I-696/M-5 interchange in Farmington Hills; M-5 (the Haggerty Connector) from that interchange north through Novi and Commerce to Pontiac Trail, a freeway past the Twelve Mile interchange and then a divided boulevard with signals and median crossovers at Thirteen and Fourteen Mile. Merge and weave crashes at the interchange, turnaround and crossover crashes on M-5, and rear-end crashes in the commuter stop-and-go. Both are MDOT’s.

**Novi Road, Grand River, and the mile roads.** Novi Road from Eight Mile to Fourteen Mile through the city’s center; Grand River’s diagonal past the Town Center, the hospital, and the 52-1 District Court; Ten, Eleven, Twelve, Thirteen, and Fourteen Mile; Beck, Haggerty, Meadowbrook, Taft, and Wixom Road. ## What is different about a Novi claim

**Coverage, and sequencing.** Michigan’s default bodily-injury liability limits are $250,000 per person and $500,000 per accident under MCL 500.3009(1), although drivers may select lower limits. Underinsured motorist coverage can be important in serious injury claims. That makes serious cases a sequencing problem: if the at-fault driver’s liability coverage is insufficient, the client’s UIM coverage may provide additional compensation, subject to the policy’s limits, offsets, consent-to-settle provisions, and other conditions. Settling without obtaining consent when the UIM policy requires it can jeopardize the UIM claim. An insurer determined under Michigan’s no-fault priority rules may pay covered medical expenses and wage-loss benefits regardless of fault, subject to applicable limits and exclusions. The Michigan Assigned Claims Plan may apply when no other applicable PIP insurer is available. The mall and freeway traffic also brings minimum-limits and out-of-state drivers, whose coverage has to be sorted out. See our [Novi car accident page](/personal-injury/auto-accidents), [no-fault insurance guide](about:blank), and [uninsured motorist page](/personal-injury/uninsured-motorist-claims).

**Road agencies.** I-96, I-275, and M-5 are MDOT’s; Haggerty, Eight Mile, Napier, Pontiac Trail, and Fourteen Mile are the Road Commission for Oakland County’s; Novi Road, Grand River, Ten through Thirteen Mile, Beck, Taft, Meadowbrook, and Wixom Road are the City of Novi’s. A crash caused by a defect in the traveled roadway may implicate the highway exception to governmental immunity, MCL 691.1402. Signal malfunctions and sight obstructions require separate legal analysis.

**Government vehicles.** Novi Police and Fire, SMART buses on Grand River and Novi Road, Novi Community Schools and Northville and Walled Lake buses, and RCOC trucks are claims under the Governmental Tort Liability Act’s motor vehicle exception (MCL 691.1405). See our [bus accident page](/personal-injury/bus-accidents).

**The 52-1 District and the Sixth Circuit.** Claims up to $25,000, including mini tort claims for vehicle damage (MCL 500.3135(3)(e), up to $3,000), are filed in the 52nd District Court, First Division, at 48150 Grand River Avenue in Novi, which serves Novi, Wixom, Walled Lake, Commerce, Highland, Milford, Lyon, South Lyon, and Wolverine Lake. Injury suits over $25,000 go to the Sixth Judicial Circuit Court at 1200 N. Telegraph Road in Pontiac. Wrongful death cases may require estate proceedings in Oakland County Probate Court, while the lawsuit itself proceeds in the court with jurisdiction. Settlements involving minors require court approval under MCR 2.420. A crash on Eight Mile or Haggerty, or with a Wayne County defendant, may allow filing in the Third Circuit in Detroit instead (MCL 600.1629).

**The hospitals.** Henry Ford Providence Novi Hospital on Grand River treats injured Novi residents; Corewell Health Farmington Hills, Henry Ford West Bloomfield, and Corewell Health William Beaumont University Hospital in Royal Oak, the county’s Level I trauma center, also treat patients with serious injuries.

## Frequently asked questions

**The other driver’s insurer offered their $250,000 policy limit. Should I take it?** Not before checking your own underinsured motorist coverage and getting your insurer’s written consent to settle. Accepting the liability limit without consent can forfeit the UIM claim under most policies. A lawyer can sequence the two claims so you recover both.

**I was hurt in a crash at the I-96/Novi Road interchange or in the Twelve Oaks parking lot. Who is responsible?** MDOT may also be responsible if a qualifying roadway-maintenance defect contributed, with a 120-day notice requirement under MCL 691.1404. In the mall lot, the other driver, and the mall owner and its contractors if the lot’s design, lighting, signage, or snow and ice contributed, under Michigan premises liability law. Mall camera footage should be requested promptly because retention periods vary.

**I was hit by a truck near the warehouses on Beck Road or Wixom Road. Who is responsible?** The driver and potentially the motor carrier, depending on the circumstances and applicable state and federal law, and possibly the warehouse operator or shipper if the loading, scheduling, or site traffic contributed. Federal regulations generally require carriers to retain hours-of-service records for at least six months, while gate-camera retention periods vary. Relevant evidence should be preserved promptly.

**Where would my Novi injury case be filed?** Claims up to $25,000, including mini tort claims, in the 52-1 District Court on Grand River in Novi; injury suits over $25,000 in the Sixth Judicial Circuit Court in Pontiac.

**I was hurt because of a signal problem or road defect in Novi. Who do I claim against?** On I-96, I-275, or M-5, MDOT, with written notice of a road defect filed in the Court of Claims within 120 days. On Novi Road, Grand River, the mile roads, or the other arterials, the City of Novi or the Road Commission for Oakland County, depending on the segment, with written notice within 120 days. A lawyer can confirm the agency from the location in a day.

**Who pays my medical bills after a crash in Novi?** Your own no-fault insurer, regardless of fault, up to your PIP level, after you file an Application for No-Fault Benefits within one year; a household member’s insurer if you have none; the Assigned Claims Plan if no policy applies.

**How much does it cost to hire you?** Nothing up front, and no fee unless we recover money for you. Michigan caps contingency fees in personal injury cases at one-third of the recovery (MCR 8.121).

**How long do I have?** Three years for most injury suits, one year to notify your no-fault insurer in writing, and as little as 120 days for a claim against the City of Novi or the RCOC.
