## Personal Injury Law Firm Serving Southfield, MI

Southfield is a city of about 76,000 at the junction of the roads that carry Oakland County to Detroit: Telegraph, Northwestern Highway, the Lodge, the Southfield Freeway, I-696, and the mile roads from Eight to Thirteen. Its office towers along Northwestern and at the Town Center bring tens of thousands of commuters in and out daily; its apartment complexes along Telegraph, Twelve Mile, and Evergreen house a renter population larger than most suburbs’; and its intersections are among the most crash-prone in the state. Telegraph at 12 Mile recorded 106 crashes in 2024, ranking third among Oakland County intersections that year, according to Michigan Traffic Crash Facts.

ATLAW is a personal injury firm in Dearborn, fifteen minutes down Telegraph or the Southfield Freeway. In 13 years the firm has handled more than 10,000 cases and recovered more than $100 million for clients across southeast Michigan, Southfield residents among them. Southfield is also, as it happens, where many Michigan personal injury firms keep their offices, in the Northwestern Highway towers; the pages those firms publish about Southfield say little about the city itself. ## Where Southfield injuries happen

**Telegraph Road (US-24).** Eight lanes through the city from Eight Mile to Thirteen Mile, with the 12 Mile intersection third on the state’s 2024 list and the 10 Mile, 11 Mile, and Northwestern intersections not far behind. Left-turn crashes across four lanes of oncoming traffic, red-light crashes, and pedestrian crashes between the apartment complexes and the retail on both sides. Telegraph is MDOT’s road.

**Northwestern Highway (M-10) and the Lodge.** The diagonal from the Lodge’s end at Eight Mile through the Town Center and the office corridor to Twelve Mile and beyond, with the I-696 interchange in the middle of it. Merging crashes at the office-tower driveways, rear-end crashes in the Lodge’s stop-and-go, and the Northwestern/Telegraph and Northwestern/Twelve Mile intersections.

**I-696.** The Reuther runs across the south of the city with interchanges at Telegraph, Northwestern/the Lodge, Southfield Road, Greenfield, and Evergreen in four miles. The I-696 Restore the Reuther project has brought significant reconstruction and traffic restrictions to the Southfield area since 2025. As of October 2026, westbound I-696 between I-75 and M-10 has reopened, while eastbound I-696 between M-10 and I-75 remains closed through late 2026. Moving violations in work zones may result in enhanced penalties, and evidence of a violation may be relevant to determining fault.

**The mile roads and the north-south arterials.** Eight Mile (M-102, the Detroit line), Nine, Ten, Eleven, Twelve, and Thirteen Mile; Evergreen, Lahser, Inkster, Greenfield, and Southfield Road. The mile roads are the Road Commission for Oakland County’s; the others are the city’s or the county’s depending on the segment. Intersection crashes at the signals and pedestrian crashes near the schools and apartment complexes.

**The office towers and the Town Center.** The Southfield Town Center, the Northwestern Highway corridor, and the Lawrence Technological University campus produce parking-structure crashes, falls on walkways and in lobbies, elevator and escalator injuries, and workplace injuries for the thousands who work in the towers. Premises claims against the tower owners and their management and security contractors, and workers’ compensation plus third-party claims for the workers. See our [premises liability](/personal-injury/premises-liability) and [workers’ compensation](/personal-injury/workers-compensation) pages.

**The apartment complexes.** Approximately 47 percent of Southfield’s occupied housing units are renter-occupied, according to the 2020–2024 American Community Survey, with apartment complexes along Telegraph, Twelve Mile, Evergreen, and Nine Mile. Falls on stairs and in parking lots, inadequate lighting and security, snow and ice, and code-violation injuries under MCL 554.139 are a steady source of claims. See our [tenant injury page](/personal-injury/tenant-injuries).

**The bars and restaurants.** Southfield’s 83 alcohol-involved crashes in 2024 follow from the restaurant and bar corridor along Northwestern and Telegraph and the city’s position between Detroit and the Woodward corridor nightlife. A drunk-driving crash adds a dram shop claim against the bar or store that served a visibly intoxicated driver, with a 120-day notice requirement (MCL 436.1801). See our [drunk driving accident page](/personal-injury/drunk-driving-accidents).

## What is different about a Southfield claim

**Coverage.** Southfield’s drivers are a mix: Oakland County commuters with full coverage and Detroit-edge traffic with the $50,000 minimum (MCL 500.3009(1)) or none. Your own no-fault insurer pays your medical bills and lost wages regardless of fault (MCL 500.3107), or the Michigan Assigned Claims Plan does if no household policy applies (MCL 500.3172, capped at $250,000); the claim against the at-fault driver requires a threshold injury (MCL 500.3135) and is worth no more than the insurance available unless you carry uninsured and underinsured motorist coverage. We review every policy in the household before we value a Southfield claim. See our [Southfield car accident page](/personal-injury/auto-accidents/southfield), [no-fault insurance guide](about:blank), and [uninsured motorist page](/personal-injury/uninsured-motorist-claims).

**Three road agencies.** MDOT has jurisdiction over state trunklines such as Telegraph (US-24), Northwestern Highway and the Lodge (M-10), Eight Mile (M-102), and I-696. The Road Commission for Oakland County maintains certain mile roads and other major roads within Southfield, while the City maintains local streets and other roads under its jurisdiction. Responsibility for individual segments of Evergreen, Lahser, and the mile roads should be checked against the City’s road-jurisdiction map. A qualifying defect in the improved portion of a public roadway may support a claim against the responsible governmental agency under MCL 691.1402. Claims based solely on traffic-signal malfunctions, inadequate signage, or obstructed sight lines generally do not fall within that highway exception. Qualifying highway-defect claims generally require written notice within 120 days under MCL 691.1404, including claims against MDOT, for which the notice must be filed with the Court of Claims as required by statute. Sidewalk falls are governed by the two-inch rule (MCL 691.1402a).

**Government vehicles.** Crashes involving Southfield Police or Fire Department vehicles, SMART buses, Southfield Public Schools buses, or RCOC trucks may support claims under the Governmental Tort Liability Act’s motor vehicle exception, MCL 691.1405, when injuries result from negligent operation of a government-owned vehicle. Certain tort claims against SMART also require written notice within 60 days under MCL 124.419. See our [bus accident page](/personal-injury/bus-accidents).

**The 46th District and the Sixth Circuit.** Claims up to $25,000, including mini tort claims for vehicle damage (MCL 500.3135(3)(e), up to $3,000), are filed in the 46th District Court at 26000 Evergreen Road, which serves Southfield and Lathrup Village. Injury suits over $25,000 go to the Sixth Judicial Circuit Court at 1200 N. Telegraph Road in Pontiac; wrongful death estates (MCL 600.2922) and minors’ settlements (MCR 2.420) to the Oakland County Probate Court on the same campus. For a crash near Eight Mile Road or involving a defendant from another county, venue must be determined under the statutory order of priority in MCL 600.1629, generally beginning with the county where the original injury occurred and where a defendant resides, conducts business, or maintains a registered office.

**The hospitals.** Henry Ford Providence Southfield Hospital, on Nine Mile Road, is one of the city’s major hospitals serving injured patients. Corewell Health William Beaumont University Hospital in Royal Oak, a Level I trauma center, Corewell Health Farmington Hills, and Detroit’s Level I trauma centers also serve patients from Southfield and surrounding communities.

## Frequently asked questions

**How dangerous is Telegraph Road in Southfield?** Telegraph at 12 Mile recorded 106 crashes in 2024, ranking third among Oakland County’s highest-crash intersections that year, based on Michigan State Police crash data. The 10 Mile, 11 Mile, and Northwestern intersections are also high-crash locations.

**I was hit in the I-696 construction zone in Southfield. Does that change the case?** It can. Drivers must follow applicable work-zone speed limits, signs, and lane restrictions. Under MCL 257.601b, moving violations committed in a work zone generally carry doubled fines, with additional penalties possible when certain violations cause injury or death. The construction contractor’s traffic-control plan, work-zone records, and available camera footage may provide important evidence. A claim against a contractor depends on its duties and whether its negligence contributed to the crash. Any claim against MDOT must also satisfy Michigan’s governmental-immunity rules and an applicable statutory exception.

**I fell in a parking structure or lobby at one of the office towers. Who is responsible?** Responsibility depends on who possessed or controlled the premises, who was responsible for maintaining the area, and whether negligence contributed to the injury. Since Kandil-Elsayed v. F & E Oil, Inc. (2023), an open-and-obvious hazard does not automatically eliminate the property owner’s duty; its obviousness may be considered when determining breach of duty and comparative fault. Incident reports and available security-camera footage may be important evidence, so it is best to request preservation promptly.

**Where would my Southfield injury case be filed?** Claims up to $25,000, including mini tort claims, in the 46th District Court on Evergreen Road; injury suits over $25,000 in the Sixth Judicial Circuit Court in Pontiac.

**I was hurt because of a bad signal or pothole. Who do I claim against?** The responsible agency depends on the road and the type of alleged defect. MDOT maintains state trunklines such as Telegraph, Northwestern, the Lodge, Eight Mile, and I-696; the Road Commission for Oakland County and City of Southfield maintain other roads according to their jurisdiction. A qualifying roadway defect may support a claim under MCL 691.1402, generally requiring written notice within 120 days under MCL 691.1404, including for MDOT claims. Traffic-signal malfunctions are different because governmental immunity generally bars claims based solely on inadequate or defective traffic-control devices.

**Who pays my medical bills after a crash in Southfield?** Your own no-fault insurer, regardless of fault, up to your PIP level, after you file an Application for No-Fault Benefits within one year; a household member’s insurer if you have none; the Assigned Claims Plan if no policy applies.

**How much does it cost to hire you?** Nothing up front, and no fee unless we recover money for you. Michigan caps contingency fees in personal injury cases at one-third of the recovery (MCR 8.121).

**How long do I have?** Three years for most injury suits, one year to notify your no-fault insurer in writing, and as little as 120 days for a claim against the City of Southfield or the RCOC.
