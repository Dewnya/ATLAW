## Personal Injury Law Firm Serving Rochester Hills, MI

Rochester Hills is a city of about 78,000 in northeastern Oakland County, crossed east to west by M-59 and north to south by Rochester Road, Adams, Crooks, Livernois, and John R, with Oakland University and its approximately 16,000 students on its northern edge, Henry Ford Rochester Hospital on University Drive, the FANUC and automotive-technology campuses along M-59 and Hamlin, and the Clinton River and Paint Creek trails threading through it. The available liability and underinsured motorist coverage can change how a serious injury claim is built; its roads are split among the state, the county, and the city, which changes who is sued for a signal or pavement defect; and its university is a state institution, which changes where a campus claim is filed. In 2024, Rochester Hills recorded 1,986 traffic crashes, with 521 people injured and two killed.

In 13 years the firm has handled more than 10,000 cases and recovered more than $100 million for clients across southeast Michigan, Rochester Hills residents among them. ## Where Rochester Hills injuries happen

**M-59.** Across the city’s middle, a freeway from I-75 to Crooks and a boulevard east of it, with interchanges and signals at Adams, Crooks, Livernois, Rochester Road, and John R, and the Rochester Road interchange carrying the retail and commuter traffic of the whole northeast county. Merge and weave crashes at the interchanges, rear-end crashes in the boulevard’s stop-and-go, and Michigan-left turnaround crashes where M-59 is divided. M-59 is MDOT’s.

**Rochester Road.** The city’s main north-south arterial, from South Boulevard and the Troy line through the M-59 interchange, the Auburn and Hamlin retail strips, and the Avon and Tienken signals to downtown Rochester; between M-59 and Tienken Road, Rochester Road is designated M-150 and is under MDOT jurisdiction. Left-turn and red-light crashes at the signals, driveway crashes at the strips, and pedestrian crashes near the restaurants and the downtown.

**Adams, Crooks, Livernois, John R, and the east-west roads.** Adams along the Oakland University and Auburn Hills edge; Crooks and Livernois through the center; John R and Dequindre along the Macomb line; Auburn, Hamlin, Avon, Tienken, Walton, and South Boulevard across. Intersection crashes at the signals, and bicycle and pedestrian crashes where the Clinton River Trail and Paint Creek Trail cross the roads at grade. Jurisdiction is split: some of these are the Road Commission for Oakland County’s and some the City of Rochester Hills’, which maintains a number of its own major roads.

**Oakland University.** Approximately 16,000 students on a campus bounded by Walton, Squirrel, Adams, and the university’s own roads, with the pedestrian crossings on Walton and Squirrel, the campus shuttles, the parking structures, the arena and athletic facilities, and the housing. Pedestrian crashes at the Walton and Squirrel crossings, campus-road and parking-structure crashes, falls, and injuries at events. Oakland University is a state university; claims against it are claims against the State of Michigan, filed in the Court of Claims with notice within six months (MCL 600.6431), and its police are state officers. Claims against students, visitors, and private contractors are ordinary claims.

**The trails.** The Clinton River Trail, the Paint Creek Trail, and the city’s pathway network, among the most used in the county, with at-grade road crossings and shared use by cyclists, runners, and walkers. A cyclist struck by a car at a crossing is covered by no-fault (MCL 500.3114, 3115); a cyclist injured by a trail defect or another trail user generally cannot claim no-fault benefits unless a motor vehicle was involved. A claim against the responsible party depends on the circumstances; governmental immunity and the recreational-use protections under MCL 324.73301 can limit liability, including for public trails. See our [bicycle accident page](/personal-injury/bicycle-accidents).

**The campuses and the retail.** FANUC America, the automotive-technology and engineering campuses along M-59 and Hamlin, Henry Ford Rochester Hospital, and the retail along Rochester Road and Adams. Parking-structure crashes, falls, and workplace injuries, where workers’ compensation is generally the exclusive remedy against the employer (MCL 418.131) but a third-party claim survives. See our [premises liability](/personal-injury/premises-liability) and [workers’ compensation](/personal-injury/workers-compensation) pages. **[CONFIRM: current major employers]**

**The neighborhoods.** Subdivision housing from the 1970s onward, owner-occupied at a high rate, with condominiums and apartment complexes along Auburn, Hamlin, and Rochester Road and senior communities along Walton and Adams. Falls on stairs and ice, condominium common-area injuries, rental-housing injuries under MCL 554.139, dog bites under MCL 287.351, and sidewalk and pathway injuries under the two-inch rule (MCL 691.1402a). ## What is different about a Rochester Hills claim

**Coverage, and sequencing.** Michigan’s default bodily-injury liability limits are $250,000 per person and $500,000 per accident under MCL 500.3009(1), although drivers may select lower limits. Available underinsured motorist coverage can be important in serious injury claims. That makes serious cases a sequencing problem. If the at-fault driver’s liability coverage is insufficient, the client’s UIM coverage may provide additional compensation, subject to the policy’s limits, offsets, consent-to-settle requirements, and other conditions. Settling without obtaining consent when the UIM policy requires it can jeopardize the UIM claim. An insurer determined under Michigan’s no-fault priority rules may pay covered medical expenses and wage-loss benefits regardless of fault, subject to the applicable coverage, limits, and exclusions (MCL 500.3107); the claim against the at-fault driver requires a threshold injury (MCL 500.3135). See our [Rochester Hills car accident page](/personal-injury/auto-accidents), [no-fault insurance guide](about:blank), and [uninsured motorist page](/personal-injury/uninsured-motorist-claims).

**Three road agencies and a state university.** M-59 and Rochester Road (M-150) between M-59 and Tienken Road are MDOT’s; other major roads are divided between the Road Commission for Oakland County and the City of Rochester Hills, depending on the segment; the campus roads are Oakland University’s, which is the State. A crash caused by a defect in the traveled roadway may implicate the highway exception to governmental immunity, MCL 691.1402. Signal malfunctions and sight obstructions require separate legal analysis. Identifying the agency from the location is an early task.

**Government vehicles.** Rochester Hills contracts its police to the Oakland County Sheriff’s Office and its fire to its own department; SMART buses run on Rochester Road and M-59; Rochester Community Schools and Avondale buses serve the city; Oakland University’s police and shuttles are the State’s. A crash involving one of these government vehicles may fall under the Governmental Tort Liability Act’s motor vehicle exception (MCL 691.1405). The proper defendant depends on who owns and operates the vehicle and the circumstances of the crash.

**The 52-3 District and the Sixth Circuit.** Claims up to $25,000, including mini tort claims for vehicle damage (MCL 500.3135(3)(e), up to $3,000), are filed in the 52nd District Court, Third Division, at 700 Barclay Circle in Rochester Hills, which serves Rochester Hills, Rochester, Auburn Hills, Oakland Township, Orion, Oxford, and Addison. Injury suits over $25,000 go to the Sixth Judicial Circuit Court at 1200 N. Telegraph Road in Pontiac. Wrongful death cases may require estate proceedings in Oakland County Probate Court, while the lawsuit is filed in the court with jurisdiction. Settlements involving minors require court approval under MCR 2.420. A crash near the Macomb County line may raise venue questions under MCL 600.1629, depending on where the injury occurred and the parties' connections to the county.

**The hospitals.** Henry Ford Rochester Hospital on University Drive (formerly Ascension Providence Rochester, renamed in the 2024 Henry Ford–Ascension transaction) treats most of Rochester Hills’ injured; Corewell Health Troy, across the Troy line, and Corewell Health William Beaumont University Hospital in Royal Oak, the county’s Level I trauma center, treat the most severe. We obtain the full record from each, including the Rochester Hills Fire Department EMS run sheet.

## Frequently asked questions

**The other driver’s insurer offered their $250,000 or $500,000 policy limit. Should I take it?** Not before checking your own underinsured motorist coverage and getting your insurer’s written consent to settle. Accepting the liability limit without consent can forfeit the UIM claim under most policies. A lawyer can sequence the two claims so you recover both.

**I was hit crossing Walton or Squirrel at Oakland University. Who do I claim against?** The driver, through no-fault for your benefits and a threshold claim for pain and suffering. If the crossing’s design, signals, or lighting contributed, the responsible road agency; if the university’s own road, shuttle, or police vehicle was involved, the State of Michigan, in the Court of Claims with a six-month notice. Students and visitors are ordinary defendants.

**I was injured on the Clinton River Trail or the Paint Creek Trail. Is that a case?** If a car hit you at a road crossing, it is a no-fault case like any other. If a trail defect or another trail user injured you, a claim may exist against a responsible party, but governmental immunity and recreational-use protections, including MCL 324.73301, may limit liability, which makes those cases harder. Photograph the location and report it the same day.

**I was hurt because of a signal problem or road defect in Rochester Hills. Who do I claim against?** On M-59 and Rochester Road (M-150) between M-59 and Tienken Road, MDOT may be responsible, with a 120-day notice requirement for qualifying highway-defect claims. On other roads, responsibility depends on the specific segment and whether MDOT, the RCOC, or the city has jurisdiction. A lawyer can confirm the agency from the location in a day.

**Where would my Rochester Hills injury case be filed?** Claims up to $25,000, including mini tort claims, in the 52-3 District Court at Barclay Circle; injury suits over $25,000 in the Sixth Judicial Circuit Court in Pontiac; claims against MDOT or Oakland University in the Court of Claims.

**Who pays my medical bills after a crash in Rochester Hills?** Your own no-fault insurer, regardless of fault, up to your PIP level, after you file an Application for No-Fault Benefits within one year; a household member’s insurer if you have none; the Assigned Claims Plan if no policy applies.

**How much does it cost to hire you?** Nothing up front, and no fee unless we recover money for you. Michigan caps contingency fees in personal injury cases at one-third of the recovery (MCR 8.121).

**How long do I have?** Three years for most injury suits, one year to notify your no-fault insurer in writing, six months for a claim against MDOT or the university, and as little as 120 days for a claim against the city or the RCOC.
