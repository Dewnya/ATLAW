Taylor is a city of about 61,500 at the center of Downriver, where I-75 and I-94 cross Telegraph Road and the Southland retail corridor on Eureka Road draws traffic from every Downriver community. Detroit Metropolitan Airport sits across the city’s western line in Romulus, and the hotels, rental-car lots, and rideshare traffic that serve it line Telegraph and the I-94 interchanges. ## Where Taylor injuries happen

**Telegraph Road (US-24).** The city’s spine, eight lanes from Van Born to the Brownstown line, past Corewell Health Taylor, the Southland corridor, Heritage Park, and the I-94 and I-75 approaches, lined with retail, hotels, and apartment complexes. The Telegraph and Eureka intersection is a major Downriver intersection; Telegraph and Goddard, Telegraph and Northline, Telegraph and Van Born, and Telegraph and Ecorse also carry substantial traffic. Left-turn and red-light crashes at the signals, rear-end crashes in the stacked turn lanes, and pedestrian crashes between the complexes and the strips. Telegraph is MDOT’s.

**Eureka Road and the Southland corridor.** From Telegraph east past Southland Center, the big-box strips, and the restaurant rows to Allen Road and I-75, with the Pardee and Allen signals and dozens of driveways. Parking-lot crashes and pedestrian strikes, driveway crashes, and the falls in the stores and lots. See our [premises liability page](/personal-injury/premises-liability).

**I-75 and I-94.** I-75 along the city’s eastern edge with the Eureka, Northline, and Goddard interchanges and the Rouge River bridge to the north, carrying the Downriver industrial freight and the through-traffic to Toledo; I-94 across the north with the Telegraph, Pelham, and Ecorse interchanges, carrying the airport traffic and the freight to the Rouge. Merge and lane-change crashes at the interchanges, rear-end crashes in the stop-and-go, and the heavy trucks that serve the Downriver plants and the airport’s cargo terminals. See our [Taylor truck accident page](/personal-injury/truck-accidents).

**Goddard, Northline, Van Born, Ecorse, Pelham, Beech Daly, Allen, Pardee, and Inkster.** The mile roads and north-south arterials, carrying neighborhood traffic to Telegraph and the interstates, past the schools and the Wayne County Community College Downriver campus. Intersection crashes at the signals and pedestrian and bicycle crashes near the schools. These are Wayne County’s roads; the subdivision streets are the city’s.

**The airport corridor.** The hotels, parking lots, rental-car returns, and shuttle and rideshare pickups along Telegraph and the I-94 interchanges that serve Detroit Metropolitan Airport. Shuttle-bus and rideshare crashes, parking-lot and hotel-premises injuries, and crashes involving out-of-state drivers in rental cars, whose no-fault coverage depends on the rental agreement and their home policy. **The neighborhoods.** Taylor’s housing is mid-century subdivision stock with about one-third of occupied housing units rented, concentrated in apartment complexes along Telegraph, Eureka, and Northline, and several manufactured-home communities. Falls on stairs and ice, rental-housing injuries under MCL 554.139, manufactured-home-park injuries, dog bites under MCL 287.351, and sidewalk injuries under the two-inch rule (MCL 691.1402a). ## What is different about a Taylor claim

**Coverage.** Downriver’s drivers carry a mix of policies, and uninsured and minimum-limits drivers are more common on Telegraph and Eureka than in the northern suburbs. Your own no-fault insurer pays your medical bills and lost wages regardless of fault (MCL 500.3107), or the Michigan Assigned Claims Plan does if no household policy applies (MCL 500.3172, capped at $250,000); the claim against the at-fault driver requires a threshold injury (MCL 500.3135) and is worth no more than the insurance available, often the $50,000 minimum (MCL 500.3009(1)), unless you carry uninsured and underinsured motorist coverage. We review every policy in the household before we value a Taylor claim. See our [Taylor car accident page](/personal-injury/auto-accidents), [no-fault insurance guide](/personal-injury/car-accident-insurance), and [uninsured motorist page](/personal-injury/uninsured-motorist-claims).

**Out-of-state drivers.** An airport city sees more rental cars and out-of-state drivers than most. A non-resident in a vehicle not registered or insured in Michigan generally has no Michigan PIP claim (MCL 500.3113(c)), and a Michigan resident hit by an out-of-state driver in a rental car is dealing with the rental company’s coverage, the driver’s home policy, and sometimes a credit-card benefit, in an order that takes sorting out.

**Three road agencies.** Telegraph, I-75, and I-94 are state trunklines under MDOT jurisdiction; other major roads may fall under Wayne County or City of Taylor jurisdiction depending on the segment, and subdivision streets are generally city-maintained. A crash caused by a defect in the traveled roadway may implicate the highway exception to governmental immunity, MCL 691.1402. Signal malfunctions and sight obstructions require separate legal analysis.

**Government vehicles.** Crashes involving Taylor Police and Fire vehicles, SMART buses, Taylor School District buses, and county road trucks may fall under the Governmental Tort Liability Act’s motor vehicle exception (MCL 691.1405). Airport shuttle claims depend on who owns and operates the vehicle, including whether the operator is the Wayne County Airport Authority or a private contractor.

**The 23rd District and the Third Circuit.** Claims up to $25,000, including mini tort claims for vehicle damage (MCL 500.3135(3)(e), up to $3,000), are filed in the 23rd District Court at 23365 Goddard Road, which serves Taylor. Injury suits over $25,000 go to the Third Judicial Circuit Court at the Coleman A. Young Municipal Center in Detroit. Wrongful death cases may require estate proceedings in Wayne County Probate Court, while the lawsuit itself is filed in the court with jurisdiction. Settlements involving minors require court approval under MCR 2.420.

**The hospitals.** Corewell Health Taylor Hospital (formerly Beaumont Taylor) on Telegraph treats injured Taylor residents; Corewell Health Dearborn, Henry Ford Wyandotte, and Detroit’s Level I trauma centers are also regional treatment options for serious injuries.

## Deadlines that apply to Taylor claims

| Claim | Deadline | Statute |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Negligence (crash, fall, dog bite) | 3 years from injury | MCL 600.5805(2) |
| Written notice to your no-fault insurer | 1 year from the crash | MCL 500.3145(1) |
| Road or sidewalk defect against the City of Taylor or Wayne County | Written notice within 120 days | MCL 691.1404 |
| Defect on Telegraph, I-75, or I-94 (MDOT) | Written notice within 120 days, filed in the Court of Claims | MCL 691.1404 |

| Medical malpractice | 2 years, 182-day notice of intent first | MCL 600.5805(8), 600.2912b |
| Injured person under 18 | Generally, one year after turning 18 if the ordinary limitations period has expired; special rules apply | MCL 600.5851(1) |

## Frequently asked questions

**Where are the crashes in Taylor?** At the Telegraph intersections, Eureka, Goddard, Northline, Van Born, and Ecorse, on the Eureka retail corridor, and at the I-75 and I-94 interchanges. In 2024, Taylor recorded 1,916 crashes, 547 injuries, and 12 deaths. Wayne County as a whole recorded 50,355 crashes, 16,458 injuries, and 177 deaths in 2024.

**The driver who hit me on Telegraph had no insurance. What now?** Your own no-fault insurer, a household member’s, or the Assigned Claims Plan pays your medical bills and lost wages regardless. Your pain-and-suffering claim depends on whether you carry uninsured motorist coverage; without it, the claim is worth what the uninsured driver can personally pay.

**I was hit by an out-of-state driver in a rental car near the airport. Who pays?** Your own no-fault insurer pays your benefits as usual. The claim against the driver involves the rental company’s coverage, the driver’s home policy, and any credit-card rental benefit, in an order that depends on the rental agreement; a non-resident driver in a vehicle not insured in Michigan generally has no Michigan PIP claim of their own.

**I fell at Southland or in a Eureka Road store. Who is responsible?** The store or mall owner and, often, the management company and the snow-removal or maintenance contractor; since Kandil-Elsayed (2023), the obviousness of the hazard is considered when evaluating breach of duty and comparative fault, rather than automatically ending the claim. Incident reports and camera footage should be requested promptly because retention periods vary and evidence may be lost.

**Where would my Taylor injury case be filed?** Claims up to $25,000, including mini tort claims, in the 23rd District Court on Goddard Road; injury suits over $25,000 in the Third Judicial Circuit Court in Detroit.

**I was hurt because of a signal problem or pothole in Taylor. Who do I claim against?** On Telegraph, I-75, or I-94, MDOT, in the Court of Claims with a six-month notice. On Eureka, Goddard, Northline, or the other mile roads, Wayne County, with written notice within 120 days. On a subdivision street, the City of Taylor, with the same 120 days.

**How much does it cost to hire you?** Nothing up front, and no fee unless we recover money for you. Michigan caps contingency fees in personal injury cases at one-third of the recovery (MCR 8.121).

**How long do I have?** Three years for most injury suits, one year to notify your no-fault insurer in writing, and as little as 120 days for a claim against the City of Taylor or Wayne County.
